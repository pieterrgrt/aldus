Whoever writes *aldus* — Dutch for *thus* — is closing something. The word stands at the end of a statement and says: that was it, this is how it was said. At the same time, it opens something. Because what was said is not yet what was meant.

## Quoting is choosing

Every quotation is a choice. You choose where to begin and where to stop, and those boundaries determine the meaning. A sentence such as

> I'm not saying it's impossible.

means something different when the next sentence is “but I wouldn't count on it” than when it is “on the contrary”.

## A short list

- What was literally said?
- In what context?
- What was left out?

Only once those three questions are answered can you say: thus.
