Anyone who follows regulation reads dozens of updates every week from the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Netherlands Authority for Consumers and Markets, the European Commission and internetconsultatie.nl. The radar reads them for you and turns them into a single overview, as a Markdown file.

## What you get

- **The script** (Node.js), which fetches the articles from your [Miniflux](https://miniflux.app) feeds and sends them to the Claude API.
- **The instructions** I use myself: what happened, who is affected, angles to write about and deadlines to keep an eye on. You adjust them in a single text file.
- **A list of Dutch and European feeds** on regulation, to get you started.
- **A step-by-step guide** to have it run every week by itself, on your laptop or your own server.

## What it costs to run

You use your own Anthropic API key. One radar costs roughly a few dozen cents, depending on the number of articles. Put a spending limit on your key, so you always know where you stand.

## What it is not

A starting point, not legal advice. Claude summarises; you judge. Always check what matters at the source.
