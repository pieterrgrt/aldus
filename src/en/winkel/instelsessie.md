Not everyone wants to read a manual. In this session we set up Claude together for your work, with your documents and your questions. The session can be in English or Dutch.

## What we do

1. **Beforehand** you send a few sentences about your work and two or three tasks you would like to hand over.
2. **In the session** we set up your Claude projects, write the instructions, and try them out on real examples.
3. **Afterwards** you get the instructions in writing and a short list of what you can still improve.

## What you need

A Claude account (preferably a paid plan) and a laptop. Confidential documents are not needed: we practise with public or anonymised texts.
