Claude gets much better when you tell it who you are, how you work and what you do *not* want. Most people start from scratch every time. This pack gives you instructions to paste into a Claude project, so you only have to get it right once.

## What you get

- **Project instructions** for [five] kinds of work: [summarising a bill or consultation, reading a policy paper critically, drafting a memo, rewriting a text in plain language, comparing sources side by side].
- **A house-style instruction** for Dutch and English text: short sentences, no bureaucratic detours, footnotes where they belong.
- **Skills for Claude Code** for those who want to go further: the same ways of working as reusable commands.
- **A short guide**: where to paste what, and how to adapt the instructions to your own practice.

## Who it is for

For people who already use Claude and notice that the answers are not quite good enough. You need a Claude account; for projects, a paid plan.

## Updates

When the ways of working improve, you get the new version by email, free of charge.
